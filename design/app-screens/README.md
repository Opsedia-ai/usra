# Usra app screens

Copied from the design canvas "Usra App Screens" (https://claude.ai/artifact/NjK7S24ioB5m91StgyT8C3) on 7 Nov 2026. The canvas is the live version.

- `variation-b/`: the chosen direction, 24 screens
- `variation-a/`: the earlier direction, 8 screens
- `english/`: 2 English checks
- `canvas.json`: screen titles and order
- `tokens.json`: colours, type, spacing and sizes

These are source files for building the app. They open properly only on the canvas: outside it, Dr. Usra images and the page script don't load.

- `merged-review.md`: the merged design review, P0 to P2, with what was rejected
- `enhanced-sample/`: the accepted direction (founder accepted on 7 Nov 2026). Where these differ from `variation-b/`, these win.
